This Privacy Policy explains what information ScoreUp (the "App") collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Overview: Offline-First and Privacy by Design

ScoreUp is built on an offline-first, on-device architecture. All user-created sports data, including player profiles, player names, avatar photos, match scores, rules, statistics, and groups, is stored exclusively on your own device using local storage.

SHS Developers does not operate servers or databases that collect, store, or receive your personal match records, player names, or profile avatars.

The following data is created and saved locally on your device:

3. Personal Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, sell, or transmit any of the following:

  • No Player or Group Names: Player names and group titles are kept 100% on your device and are never sent to our servers or third-party analytics.
  • No Personal Identifiers: Your real name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • No Precise Location Data: We never request or access GPS or real-time geolocation.
  • No Photo Uploads: Avatar pictures selected from your library stay on your device.
  • No Financial / Payment Data: The App has no in-app purchases or paid features.

4. Camera and Photo Library Access

The App may request access to your device's camera or photo library only if you choose to set an avatar photo for a player profile:

  • Optional: Setting an avatar photo is completely optional. Default initials or icons can be used instead.
  • On-demand: The permission prompt appears only when you tap the avatar selector.
  • Stored Locally: Any chosen photos are stored in the App's private local directory and are never uploaded to any cloud server or third party.

You can modify or revoke photo and camera permissions at any time through your device's operating system settings.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To provide access to match-creation tickets without monetary purchases, the App includes optional rewarded video advertisements served by Google AdMob (react-native-google-mobile-ads).

  • Non-Personalized Ads: We configure ad requests in non-personalized mode (requestNonPersonalizedAdsOnly: true). This requests contextual ads rather than ads based on your historical activity across other apps or websites.
  • Ticket System: New installations receive 20 default tickets. Users may optionally choose to watch a rewarded video ad to earn additional tickets (5 tickets per ad). Watching ads is optional.
  • Data Collected by Ad Networks: When an ad is requested or viewed, Google AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA), coarse/approximate location (derived from IP address), and ad interaction telemetry (e.g., whether the ad was watched to completion). This collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Managing Ad Tracking on Your Device:

  • Android: Open Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
  • iOS: Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable tracking requests, or Apple Advertising → toggle off Personalised Ads.

6. App Analytics & Diagnostics (PostHog)

We use PostHog (PostHog, Inc., hosted securely in the United States) to collect anonymized telemetry and operational events. This allows us to monitor app reliability, diagnose performance bottlenecks, and understand feature adoption.

What PostHog Collects:

  • App Lifecycle Events: Application opened, resumed, or backgrounded.
  • Feature & Screen Navigation: Screen views (e.g., QuickMatchScreen, StatsScreen), sports formats selected, timer starts, and match completion events.
  • Aggregated Game Counters: Sport type (e.g., football, badminton), score counter increments, and match durations.
  • Technical Device Metadata: Device model, operating system version, app version (0.26.x), network connectivity status, and approximate country/region (inferred from IP address).

What PostHog NEVER Collects:

  • Player names, group names, and match participant identities are strictly excluded from all PostHog event payloads.
  • No user content, notes, custom rules text, or avatar images are sent to PostHog.
  • PostHog data is never sold or used for cross-app advertising profiling.

For more information on PostHog's data handling practices, visit the PostHog Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

ScoreUp is designed as a family-friendly sports scoring tool suitable for players and coaches of all ages. Because:

  1. No user accounts or personal profiles are created,
  2. All player names and match statistics remain strictly on your local device, and
  3. Telemetry is anonymized and never includes names or personal details,

we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If a child watches an optional rewarded ad, that interaction is subject to Google AdMob's child-directed policies and parent/guardian oversight.

8. No Account or Registration Required

You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address or password to use ScoreUp. There are no cloud credentials, and your application data is bound directly to your device instance.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all your match and profile data is stored locally on your device, you maintain full control over its retention:

  • Delete Individual Records: Delete individual players, groups, or match logs using the delete options within the App.
  • Reset All Data: Use the "Reset all Data" button in Settings to purge all local databases.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling ScoreUp immediately and permanently deletes all locally stored data, players, matches, and ticket balances.

Note: Because SHS Developers does not hold your data on any server, we cannot recover or delete records on your behalf.

10. Data Security

Local match records and player data are protected by your device's operating system security (such as app sandboxing, device encryption, and lock screen protections). We recommend keeping your device updated and securing it with a PIN, passcode, or biometrics.
