This Privacy Policy explains what information ScoreUp (the "App") collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Overview: We Do Not Collect Your Data

ScoreUp is an offline, on-device app. All data you enter, including player names, avatar photos, match records, scores, statistics, and preferences, is stored exclusively on your own device using the device's local file system. SHS Developers does not operate any servers, databases, or cloud services that receive or store your personal data.

The only exception is optional rewarded advertising served by Google AdMob, described in Section 5 (Advertising and Third-Party Services).

2. What Data the App Stores (On Your Device)

The following data is created and stored locally on your device only:

3. Data We Do Not Collect

SHS Developers does not collect, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Location data (GPS or network-based) collected by the App itself
  • Browsing or search history
  • Device contacts, calendar, or microphone
  • Any analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics from within the App itself
  • Payment or financial information (the App has no paid features)

Information collected by Google AdMob when you view advertisements is described in Section 5. That information is collected by Google, not by SHS Developers.

4. Camera and Photo Library Access

The App may request access to your camera or photo library only if you choose to add an avatar photo to a player profile. This access is:

  • Optional: avatars are not required to use any feature of the App
  • On-demand: the App only accesses your photos when you actively tap the avatar button
  • Local only: selected photos are stored on your device and are never uploaded anywhere

You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's system settings.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

This app uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC, to display advertisements. Users may choose to watch optional rewarded video advertisements to earn additional tickets. AdMob may use device identifiers, cookies, and location data to serve personalized and non-personalized ads.

Google AdMob collects information such as your Advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data to personalize ad experiences and prevent ad fraud.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google AdMob and its partners may collect the following:

  • Advertising ID: the Android Advertising ID (AAID) or the Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), which Google uses to show personalized ads and to measure and analyze ad performance
  • IP address and approximate location (country or region level, derived from the IP address)
  • Ad interaction data, such as whether an ad was viewed, clicked, or completed
  • Device information, such as device type, operating system, and language
  • Cookies and similar technologies used for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention

This data is collected by Google, not by SHS Developers, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy. Watching rewarded ads is entirely optional. You can use the App's features with your 25 default tickets without ever watching an ad.

5.1 Your Choices and Opt-Out

You can choose not to see personalized ads. Google provides the following official resources:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Ads Settings (opt out of personalized advertising)

You can also limit ad tracking directly in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or opt out of Ads personalization)
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off Personalised Ads, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to manage app tracking permissions

Opting out does not remove ads. It means the ads you see will not be personalized based on your Advertising ID.

6. Children's Privacy

ScoreUp is designed for users of all ages and does not require any account registration or personal information to function. Because all App data stays on-device and SHS Developers does not receive any user data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If a child under 13 uses the rewarded ad feature, the parent or guardian is responsible for supervising that interaction and reviewing Google's privacy policy. We recommend that parents prevent younger children from watching rewarded ads.

7. No Account or Registration Required

The App does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or sign in. There is no login, no password, and no user profile stored on any server.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

All App data is retained on your device until you delete it. You can delete your data at any time:

  • Delete specific records: use the in-app delete options for players, groups, or matches
  • Delete all data: uninstall the App from your device. This permanently removes all locally stored data

SHS Developers cannot retrieve, restore, or delete your App data on your behalf because we have no access to it. Data held by Google through AdMob can be managed through the Google links in Section 5.1.

9. Data Security

Because your App data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device protected with a PIN, fingerprint, or other lock to prevent unauthorized access to your match records and player data.

10. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): serves advertisements within the App and collects device identifiers and related data as described in Section 5. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo: the App is built using the Expo framework. Expo may collect anonymized crash or performance diagnostics at the platform level. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any other third-party analytics SDKs within the App.
