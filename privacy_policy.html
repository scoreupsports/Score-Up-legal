This Privacy Policy explains what information ScoreUp (the "App") collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Overview: We Do Not Collect Your Data

ScoreUp is a fully offline, on-device app. All data you enter, including player names, avatar photos, match records, scores, statistics, and preferences, is stored exclusively on your own device using the device's local file system. SHS Developers does not operate any servers, databases, or cloud services that receive or store your personal data.

2. What Data the App Stores (On Your Device)

The following data is created and stored locally on your device only:

Data TypeExamplesWhere Stored
Player profilesNames, optional avatar photosYour device's local storage
Match recordsScores, sport, participants, timestampsYour device's local storage
StatisticsPer-player stats by sport and groupYour device's local storage
GroupsGroup names and member listsYour device's local storage
App preferencesOnboarding status, last used format/sportYour device's local storage
Ticket balanceNumber of tickets remainingYour device's local storage

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

SHS Developers does not collect, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Location data (GPS or network-based)
  • Browsing or search history
  • Device contacts, calendar, or microphone
  • Any analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics from within the App itself
  • Payment or financial information (the App has no paid features)

4. Camera and Photo Library Access

The App may request access to your camera or photo library only if you choose to add an avatar photo to a player profile. This access is:

  • Optional: avatars are not required to use any feature of the App
  • On-demand: the App only accesses your photos when you actively tap the avatar button
  • Local only: selected photos are stored on your device and are never uploaded anywhere

You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's system settings.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Rewarded Ads)

To earn additional tickets, users may choose to watch optional rewarded video advertisements provided by third-party ad networks. When you watch a rewarded ad, the third-party ad provider may collect:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID / IDFA)
  • Approximate location (country/region level, derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (e.g. whether the ad was completed)
  • Device type, operating system, and language

This data is collected by the ad network, not by SHS Developers, and is governed by the ad network's own privacy policy. Watching rewarded ads is entirely optional. You can use the App's features using your 25 default tickets without ever watching an ad.

If you wish to limit ad tracking, you can do so in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off Personalised Ads

6. Children's Privacy

ScoreUp is designed for users of all ages and does not require any account registration or personal information to function. Because all data stays on-device and SHS Developers does not receive any user data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If a child under 13 uses the rewarded ad feature, the parent or guardian is responsible for supervising that interaction and reviewing the ad network's privacy policy. We recommend that parents disable rewarded ads for younger children by not permitting ad-watching.

7. No Account or Registration Required

The App does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or sign in. There is no login, no password, and no user profile stored on any server.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

All data is retained on your device until you delete it. You can delete your data at any time:

  • Delete specific records: use the in-app delete options for players, groups, or matches
  • Delete all data: uninstall the App from your device. This permanently removes all locally stored data

SHS Developers cannot retrieve, restore, or delete your data on your behalf because we have no access to it.

9. Data Security

Because your data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device protected with a PIN, fingerprint, or other lock to prevent unauthorized access to your match records and player data.

10. Third-Party Services

The App is built using the Expo framework. Expo may collect anonymized crash or performance diagnostics at the platform level. Please refer to Expo's Privacy Policy for details. We do not use any additional third-party analytics SDKs within the App.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this document and, for significant changes, notify you through an in-app notice. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding your personal data, such as the right to access, correct, or delete it. Because all your data is stored only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly at any time by using the in-app delete features or by uninstalling the App.
